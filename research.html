RBX Labs Research & Practice Lab

Study the work. Build the practice.

As AI moves from prompts to agents and operational systems, the Lab studies what is delegated, what evidence supports the result, what remains human-owned, and who is accountable. We turn those questions into research, workshops, prototypes, evaluations, and sponsored pilots through RBX Labs.
