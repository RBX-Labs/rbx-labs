Workshop path

From tutorial concept to one team workflow

1 Choose

One workflow, intended use, and decision owner.

2 Map

Sources, tools, permissions, handoffs, excluded actions, and stop conditions.

3 Gate

Pass, review, and escalate criteria for accuracy, completeness, source quality, uncertainty, policy fit, and operational usefulness.

4 Route

Escalation matrix for conflicting sources, stale evidence, privacy concerns, safety-critical implications, tool failure, and failed validation.

5 Record

Minimum audit record and decision trace.

6 Implement

30-day plan with owners, evidence gaps, pilot scope, monitoring signals, and review cadence.

Outputs

Minimum audit record

Next actions, owners, evidence gaps, pilot scope, monitoring signals, and review cadence.
