Account controls

Delete your Network Guardian account

You can request deletion of your Network Guardian account and associated personal data without uninstalling the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address associated with your Network Guardian account.
  2. Use the subject line Network Guardian Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the Google account email used to sign in.

Email an account deletion request

What happens next

We verify the request, remove or anonymize the account information and account-linked contributions that are not required to be retained, and reply when the request is complete. Limited records may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

Before you request deletion

Deleting an account may remove access to account-linked scan history, saved data, and community contribution history. Guest scans stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the app's local data or uninstalling the app.

Data that may be retained

Security logs, fraud-prevention records, legally required transaction records, deletion-compliance records, and encrypted backups may be retained for the period required for those purposes. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

For more information, read the Privacy Policy.
