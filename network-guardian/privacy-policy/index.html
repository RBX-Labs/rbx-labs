Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RBX Labs collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data in connection with the Network Guardian mobile application, website pages, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. Who We Are

Network Guardian is operated by RBX Labs. For privacy questions or rights requests, contact: privacy@rbx-labs.io.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account information such as name, email, and organization details (if provided).
  • Device and app metadata, including OS version, app version, identifiers, and diagnostics.
  • Network scan and trust-signal data, such as SSID/BSSID context, risk indicators, and confidence signals.
  • Usage telemetry, including feature usage, session timestamps, and performance/error logs.
  • Support communications and any information you submit directly to us.

3. How We Use Data

  • Provide Wi-Fi trust scoring, risk alerts, and security insights before connection.
  • Maintain service integrity, detect abuse, and investigate incidents.
  • Improve model quality, product performance, and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our contractual terms.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where required by applicable law, we rely on: consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, and legal obligations.

5. Data Sharing

  • Service providers that host infrastructure, analytics, security, and support tools.
  • Professional advisors, regulators, or law enforcement when legally required.
  • Successors in a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset transfer.

We do not sell personal information for money.

6. Retention

We retain data for only as long as necessary for the purposes listed above, including operational continuity, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and contract enforcement.

7. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No system can be guaranteed fully secure.

8. International Transfers

Data may be processed in countries outside your own. We apply appropriate safeguards when required by law.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, or receive a portable copy of your personal data. To exercise rights, email privacy@rbx-labs.io.

Account deletion instructions are available at network-guardian/account-deletion/. Selective data deletion instructions are available at network-guardian/data-deletion/.

10. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or higher age as required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Updated versions will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
