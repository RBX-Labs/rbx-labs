Privacy Policy

Network Guardian helps you understand the trust and health of Wi-Fi networks before you rely on them. This policy explains what the app collects, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: August 6, 2026

Who is responsible

Network Guardian is operated by RBX Labs. For privacy questions or requests, contact rishabh@rbx-labs.io.

Information we collect

Account information

If you sign in with Google, we receive the account identifier, email address, display name, and authentication token claims needed to create and secure your Network Guardian session. We do not receive your Google password.

Network and diagnostic information

When you run a scan, the app may process the Wi-Fi name (SSID), access-point identifier (BSSID), signal strength, channel, security mode, gateway and DNS information, public IP/ASN information, latency, reachability, TLS and DNS test results, and related diagnostic metadata.

Location and device information

With permission, the app may use approximate device location to associate network observations with a geographic area. The app also generates a device hash and records app version and platform details to help prevent duplicate or abusive community submissions.

Background and notification information

If you enable Background Mode, the app uses Android foreground-service and notification capabilities to run scheduled network checks and show service status. You can disable this mode or revoke the related permissions in Android settings.

How we use information

  • Provide local Wi-Fi diagnostics and trust explanations.
  • Authenticate your account and protect community contributions.
  • Build aggregate network reputation and reliability signals.
  • Detect duplicate, abusive, or anomalous submissions.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app and API.

Sharing and service providers

Network Guardian uses Google Sign-In for authentication and Google Cloud hosting for its API and database infrastructure. We share information with these providers only as needed to provide authentication, hosting, storage, security, and service operations. We do not sell personal information and the app currently does not use advertising SDKs.

Community data

Authenticated scans may contribute to aggregate network reputation. Community results are intended as advisory signals, not guarantees of safety. Guest scans remain local and are not submitted as community contributions.

Retention and security

We retain account and contribution data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so protect your account and device.

Your choices

  • Use local guest scans without creating an account.
  • Revoke location, Wi-Fi, notification, or background permissions in Android settings.
  • Sign out of the app.
  • Request account deletion through our account deletion page.

Children

Network Guardian is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will identify the current version.
