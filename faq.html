What is RBX Labs?

RBX Labs is a founder-led applied AI studio with a Research & Practice Lab. It builds AI products and workflows, delivers practical training, and runs applied research on AI judgment and runtime trust.

Who does RBX Labs work with?

RBX Labs works with founders, product teams, operators, universities, public-interest organizations, and AI or open-source ecosystems that need practical execution or applied research.

What outcomes does RBX Labs create?

Typical outcomes include shipped AI workflows, measurable efficiency gains, improved activation, and clearer launch execution.

Does RBX Labs deliver AI training and public teaching?

Yes. RBX Labs runs workflow-specific workshops, sprints, adoption programs, public talks, and teaching.

How does RBX Labs approach trustworthy AI?

RBX Labs builds evaluation, human oversight, monitoring, escalation paths, permissions, and replayable evidence into the operating workflow.

What is the Research & Practice Lab?

It is a named program inside RBX Labs that turns applied questions about AI judgment, delegated thinking, runtime trust, and evaluation into research, workshops, prototypes, case studies, and sponsored pilots.

What does the Research & Practice Lab study?

The Lab studies how people should delegate work to capable AI without outsourcing judgment, and how systems should expose evidence, evaluation, permissions, monitoring, escalation, and accountability.

What does a Lab collaboration produce?

A collaboration produces a defined artifact such as a research report, workshop, evaluation method, prototype, case study, or sponsored pilot with a public or private output.
