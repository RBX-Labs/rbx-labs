AI Judgment Workshop

Help your team use AI without outsourcing judgment. In 90 minutes, participants map what AI may do, what must be verified, when to escalate, and who owns the result.

Plan a workshop

Decide

Set the AI role, delegation boundary, stakes, privacy constraints, policy limits, and human owner.

Question

Identify a specific failure that could make the output wrong, incomplete, biased, or misleading.

Verify

Choose an independent source, test, comparison, or qualified reviewer and record what changed.

Reflect

Explain the important idea in your own words and apply it to a new example or counterexample.

Own

Choose the responsible next action: disclose, revise, reject, escalate, or abstain.

What your team receives

A completed decision trace, delegation/verification/escalation map, responsibility map, and 30-day team experiment. Includes a 30-minute preparation call and a written action summary within five business days.

Training and workflow design, not legal advice, compliance certification, employee-performance rating, or a validated psychological assessment. Do not use this workshop to rank employees or make high-stakes decisions about people.
