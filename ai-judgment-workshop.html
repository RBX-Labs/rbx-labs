A five-step operating rule for the messy middle.

Policies tell people what is allowed. Judgment helps them decide what to do when the answer is plausible, the stakes are unclear, and the system is confident.

01

Decide

Set the AI role, boundary, stakes, privacy, and human owner.

02

Question

Name the specific failure that could make the output wrong.

03

Verify

Check with independent evidence, a test, or qualified review.

04

Reflect

Explain what changed and transfer the idea to a new case.

05

Own

Disclose, revise, reject, escalate, or abstain.

Leave with something the team can use tomorrow.

The session is built around one real workflow and sanitized examples. No abstract AI inspiration. No employee scorecard.
